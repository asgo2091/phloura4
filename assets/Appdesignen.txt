This app/program is a diary for your growing. In it, you can make notes about what you've done, what plants you have, and where you're growing.
<br><br>
This way you can go back to the previous years and see what you did, mistakes and successful attempts. You can also make notes about weather and other circumstances that affected.
<br><br>
The program is structured with four registers, or lists, which together provide a picture of what has happened.
<br><br>
One list contains the plants that you grow or plan to try.
<br><br>
One list contains the locations you use, such as greenhouse, open field, hotbed, etc.
<br><br>
The third list should contain the actions you take in your crops. By having a list with specific names for the actions, you can search and sort easily without risking ambiguity or confusion.
<br><br>
The fourth list is the most important, the one where you record what happened. You enter the date and then a description of the event, such as frost, drought and other things that affected your crops.
But mainly you describe sowing, replanting, harvesting and other things that you do. To such a note you link the location and plants from the other registers. You can go back here later to see what happened, what you should do again and what is best to avoid.
<br><br>
Good luck!
<br><br>
<b>ATTENTION!</b> The program stores data in the phone. In the event of a breakdown of the phone, all notes are lost. A backup routine protects against this.
