Here you decide what you want a compilation of your notes to look like.
<br><br> 
To begin with, you decide whether you want to be sorted by plant, place or action. 
For example, if you decide on a plant, you can then choose which one. 
You choose from your own list of plants.
<br>
You can then choose which time should be included. 
If you do not set a time, all notes you have made for that plant will be displayed.
<br><br>
When you press continue, a list will be displayed as you have decided and you can print it if you wish.
